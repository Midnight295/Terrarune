A mod that adds various DELTARUNE related vanities!

Currently adds vanity items based off of the Fun Gang members! And Berdly.

Recipes:
-Pie Knife: 15 Iron Bars and 3 Fruit at an Anvil
-Tasty Chalk: 35 Stone Blocks and 15 Clay Blocks at an Anvil
-Fluffy Hat: 15 Silk at a Loom.
-Wristwatch: Silver Watch and 35 Snow Blocks at a Workbench.
-A+ Test: 15 Silk at a Loom.
-Dusty CRT: 10 Iron Bars and 15 Silk at an Anvil.
